# OPDS Plus

OPDS Plus is a KOReader plugin for browsing online book catalogs (OPDS feeds) with covers, in a list or a grid,
and downloading books straight to your e-reader. It reads OPDS 1 and OPDS 2.0 catalogs and updates itself.
